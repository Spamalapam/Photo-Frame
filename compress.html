How to use this: Pick a batch of photos, wait for them to compress (in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere), then either tap each card's "Get" button or "Download all" to save them. Then go to github.com/Spamalapam/Photo-Frame/tree/main/photos, tap Add file → Upload files, and drop the compressed photos in. Default settings (2000px JPEG q=85) typically take a 10 MB phone photo down to ~1 MB with no visible quality loss on a 12.4" tablet.
